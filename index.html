Power-Ups
Words cleared
    1. Arrow Keys / WASD Keys / Swipe to move letters.
    2. Left and Right arrow keys switch vertically between horizontal slices. Up and Down arrow keys twists the selected horizontal slice
    3. W and S keys switch horizontally between vertical slices. A and D keys twists the selected vertical slice.
    4. Press 'Space' to rotate to different faces of the cube.
    5. Press Enter to submit words when highlighted.
    6. When stuck, click 'Lock Tile' to lock a selected letter in place, 'Lock Word' to lock a potential word in place, 'Freeze Slice' to lock a selected slice (vertical or horizontal) in place, 'Freeze Face' to lock the current face in place, and Reshuffle to reset the cube.
    7. Toggle on and off "Show Ready Word" to enable/disable highlight.
    8. Wildcard(?) spawns randomly on the grid. When part of a valid word, click the tile directly to choose any letter to complete your word.
    9. Rare double-letter tiles (like EE, OO, TT) help build longer, higher-scoring words quickly.
    10. Rare letters (Q, Z, X, J, K, V) grant a 2x Score Multiplier to the word.
    11. Forming a horizontal and vertical word that intersect triggers a Cross Multiplier that doubles the combined score of both words.
    12. Back-to-back Cross matches increases your Combo Multiplier (2x, 4x, etc.).
    13. Words that overlap into a different face grant a 2x Score Multiplier
    14. Cross matches made with overlapping words grant a 2x Score Multiplier on top.
